Play the daily Wikipedia racing game

WikiGame challenges you to reach one Wikipedia article from another using the links inside each page. Play the daily pair, try a random game, or choose your own starting and target articles.

Press Begin to start the timer, then follow article links until you reach the target. Every move counts as a hop, including going back. At the end, review your route, time, and hop count and copy a result to share.

A bot attempts the same route to set a par score, but its route is not guaranteed to be shortest. Daily results are saved in this browser. Playing requires JavaScript and access to Wikipedia; if the bot cannot find a route, you can still play without a par.
